What's going on here?

The point in time controls shape growth, rotation, writing, light and transitions together. The surface uses spatial curves with a perspective projection. Brightness and light traces follow their own curves. The same point in time generates the same model state as the slide forwards and backwards.

For Morrowind, this will become building blocks for magic, menu transitions and material light. Graphics & Games is linked.

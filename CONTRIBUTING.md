# Contributing / Beiträge

Before submitting a contribution, identify its human author(s), source,
applicable licence or permission, and any third-party material. Mark
AI-assisted content as AI-assisted and describe the human review or changes.
Do not include private data, credentials, or material whose rights are unclear.

Submitting content does not by itself assign copyright to Juri Janovski or
Juri Halveth. Contributors must have authority to grant the rights required by
the applicable path and version in [LICENSES.md](LICENSES.md), and must also
consider GitHub's current terms for contributions to a repository that contains
a licence notice. The repository does not request an implied exclusive rights
transfer. Any broader or exclusive grant requires a separate written agreement
that identifies the work, version, rights, parties, scope, and acceptance.

If the source, author, third-party licence, AI provenance, or permission cannot
be established, disclose the gap and hold the material out of the repository
until it is resolved. See [Rights and provenance](docs/RIGHTS-AND-PROVENANCE.md)
for the evidence record and review steps.
